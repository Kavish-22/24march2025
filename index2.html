IPL 2025: Ishan Kishan finds redemption with debut SRH ton after tough times

On his debut for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Ishan Kishan's first IPL hundred took his team almost to the highest total in IPL history, which was too much for Rajasthan Royals to challenge.

It’s the impact players who often make the game-changing contributions in games between teams of roughly equal strength. Opposition captains are aware of what the consistent ones bring to the table, but if mavericks like Ishan Kishan have their day, it can leave them scratching their heads. On his debut for Sunrisers Hyderabad, the left-hander’s first IPL hundred took his team almost to the highest total in IPL history, which was too much for Rajasthan Royals to challenge. However, at one stage when Sanju Samson and Dhruv Jurel were piling on the pressure during the chase, it seemed Sunrisers would need every one of their 286 runs. And it was only Ishan’s onslaught throughout the innings – 106 not out in 47 balls with 11 fours and six maximums – that got them there after Travis Head had provided a typically belligerent start with a 31-ball 67 and Abhishek Sharma, Nitish Kumar Reddy and Heinrich Klaasen played useful cameos.
